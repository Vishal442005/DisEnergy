# Cover Letter Draft — *Pattern Recognition* (Elsevier)

Dear Editor-in-Chief and Editorial Board of *Pattern Recognition*,

Please consider our manuscript, **"DisEnergy: Multi-Scale Dirichlet--Wasserstein 3D Energy Manifolds for Box-Free Segmentation and Topological Classification,"** for publication in *Pattern Recognition*.

### Summary of Contributions
1. **Unified Feed-Forward Operator:** We formulate DisEnergy ($\mathcal{D}_s$), a dimensionally homogeneous multi-scale divergence operator coupling local Dirichlet gradient energy and block dispersion with closed-form Gaussian / Bures Wasserstein-2 transport down to the $1 \times 1$ pixel limit.
2. **Formal Analytical Guarantees:** We prove five theorems establishing exact affine photometric invariance, noise-bias cancellation with $O(1/w_s)$ stochastic attenuation, $O(N \cdot |\mathcal{N}_r|)$ computational complexity, cubical topological bottleneck stability, and iso-mean texture separability—each backed by automated numerical verification tests.
3. **Box-Free Dual-Task 3D Manifold & Cross-Domain Rigor:** Using Moran's-$I$ regularized inverse-entropy fusion, DisEnergy produces a 3D elevation manifold driving both crest-snapping topological watershed masks and cubical persistent homology ($H_0/H_1/H_2$) classification across biomedical, industrial, remote sensing, and natural scene domains.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors approve the submission and declare no competing interests.
